The story

One studio.
Two coasts.

How a Barcelona workshop ended up opening an install bay in South Florida — and what came with it.

  1. 2023

    SERRES opens in Barcelona

    A wrap and paint-protection studio built around a single conviction: almost every bad film job is a bad prep job. Decontaminate properly, measure the paint before you touch it, keep dust out of the bay — and the film does the rest.

    The work that followed was mostly the difficult kind: widebody Porsches, satin wraps over long panels, full-front film. It is all in the gallery, shot in the bay it was finished in.

  2. Today

    The protocol, written down

    Not house style — a checklist. Paint thickness gauged at intake, on every panel. Air filtered before it reaches a car. Gloss read before and after correction, so the improvement is a number instead of an opinion. Every stage photographed.

    The film list is two: 3M Scotchgard Pro Series 200 and NAR H190. Both self-healing TPU, both with a real manufacturer warranty behind them — one flagship, one that does the same job for less. Cheap TPH film is not on it, and will not be.

  3. 2026

    Boca Raton

    Florida asks more of paint than Barcelona does: year-round UV, salt air, love bugs, and rock chips off I-95 and Glades Road. The protocol was built for exactly that kind of punishment, so it crosses the Atlantic intact rather than being rebuilt from scratch.

    The exact street address is announced in October 2026. The opening runs in two stages:

    Stage one · detailing November 13, 2026 Detailing services open: interior details and paint correction, with gloss measured in and out.
    Stage two · full PPF December 8, 2026 Full paint protection film launch, with the filtered install bay commissioned and both film tiers available.

The method

The SERRES
protocol.

Six fixed steps, on every car, at every price. None of them is an upsell and none of them is skipped when the day runs long.

  1. 01

    Paint thickness gauged at intake

    Before anything touches the car, every panel is read with an electronic thickness gauge and the numbers are recorded. Thin paint, filler and previous respray work show up here — not halfway through a correction pass.

  2. 02

    HEPA-filtered bay

    The air in the install bay is HEPA-filtered before it reaches the car. A speck of dust trapped under paint protection film or vinyl is not a flaw you polish out later — it is permanent for the life of the film.

  3. 03

    Anti-static prep

    Clean panels still pull dust out of the air. The anti-static prep step neutralizes the charge on the surface so the paint stops attracting the exact particles the filtered bay just removed.

  4. 04

    Filtered-air dry

    After the final rinse the car is dried with filtered air, not towels. Nothing is dragged across decontaminated paint, and water is blown out of seams, badges and mirror bases before it can creep back under a freshly laid edge.

  5. 05

    Gloss measurement

    Gloss is measured on the same panels, at the same points, before and after correction. Both readings go in your handover record. It is the difference between us telling you the paint improved and us showing you by how much.

  6. 06

    Photo documentation

    Intake, mid-process and handover, photographed under the studio lights. The set goes to you with the car — which is also the record of the condition it arrived in.

Why is a Barcelona studio opening in Boca Raton?

Because South Florida is harder on paint than Barcelona is: year-round UV, salt air, love bugs and rock chips off I-95. The protocol we built in Barcelona was designed for exactly that kind of punishment, so we are bringing it over intact instead of starting again. Same two films, same warranty terms: Manufacturer film warranty (up to 10 years) + 1-year Serres installation warranty.

When does the Boca Raton studio open?

In two stages. Detailing services open on November 13, 2026, and the full paint protection film launch follows on December 8, 2026. The exact street address is announced in October 2026.

What does the SERRES protocol actually change?

Six fixed steps on every car, whatever it costs: paint thickness gauged at intake, a HEPA-filtered bay, anti-static prep, a filtered-air dry, gloss measured before and after correction, and a photo record of every stage. None of it is an optional extra and none of it is reserved for expensive cars.
