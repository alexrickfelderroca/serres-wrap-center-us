01Who these terms are between

These terms apply between SERRES Wrap Center — the vehicle protection studio opening in the Boca Raton area — and you, the person booking work on a vehicle. Booking a job, or paying a deposit, means these terms apply to that job.

They cover paint protection film, wraps, window tint and detailing. They do not cover anything you buy from our Barcelona studio, which trades under Spanish law and its own terms.

02Quotes, prices and tax

Every price on this site is published, and every one of them is a starting price for a real, standard car — not a teaser you can never actually book.

“From” pricing = real price for a standard sedan in good condition — no bait pricing.

Published price list · terms

What moves a price is the car and its condition: panel count and size, paint that needs correcting before anything is sealed onto it, previous film or wrap that has to come off, and trim that has to come apart. That is why a published price becomes a firm price only once we have seen the vehicle — by photos first, then in the bay before work starts.

The firm quote we give you is the price of the job. If we find something on the car that changes it, we stop and tell you before carrying on; work you have not agreed to is never added to an invoice.

Prices exclude FL sales tax.

Published price list · terms

Florida sales tax is added on the invoice at the rate in force on the day. The full published list is on the pricing page.

03Booking and the 30% deposit

Booking secured with a 30% deposit.

Published price list · terms

A date in the bay is held with a deposit of 30% of the agreed price. The deposit is part of the price, not an extra: it comes off the final invoice, and the balance is due when you collect the car.

Until the deposit is paid, a date is a conversation, not a booking. Film in particular is cut for a specific vehicle in a specific slot — the deposit is what lets us commit both to you.

04Founders Club reservations

Before the studio opens, a limited number of places can be reserved in advance. A reservation is not a booking of a specific date — it holds your place and the price list, and we come back to you with a quote and a date.

  • Refundable until work begins. The reservation deposit is returned in full if you change your mind at any point before we start work on the car.
  • Applied to the final invoice. It is not a fee. Once the job is done it comes off what you owe.
  • The discount is locked to the price list on the day you book. If our prices rise before your car comes in, yours does not.
  • Limited to the first 30 cars. When those places are taken, the offer closes.
  • One car per reservation. A reservation covers a single vehicle and is not transferable to another car.

The reservation page opens before the studio does. Until then nothing on this site takes a payment of any kind.

05Warranty

Manufacturer film warranty (up to 10 years) + 1-year Serres installation warranty.

Published price list · terms

Two separate things, and it is worth knowing which is which.

  • The film warranty is the manufacturer's — up to ten years, depending on the film you choose. It covers the film as a product, on the manufacturer's own terms. The claim is against the manufacturer, and we handle it with them on your behalf.
  • The installation warranty is ours — one year on the work itself. If an edge lifts, a seam opens or a panel shows an installation defect within that year, we put it right at our cost.

Neither warranty is damage cover: impact, road debris that goes through the film, chemical or mechanical damage from washing, or a repaint underneath are not installation defects. Nor does the installation warranty cover a car we did not install on.

06Your vehicle while it is with us

Film and coating work needs a clean, controlled bay and, on longer jobs, an overnight cure. Your car stays with us for the length of the job, and we tell you that length before you leave it.

Bring the car with personal belongings removed. We work on the vehicle, not on its contents.

Plans change. Tell us as early as you can and we will move the date if the slot can be filled.

We hold ourselves to the same standard: if something in the studio means we cannot start on the agreed day, you hear it from us as soon as we know — not when you arrive.

We photograph most cars before and after — partly as a record of the vehicle's condition when it arrived, partly because the work is how the studio is judged.

If you would rather your car never appeared anywhere public, say so when you book and it will not. Plates are not published.

09Payment and Square

No payment is taken on this website. Deposits and invoices are handled by Square, on Square's own pages, and we never see or store your card number.

The balance is due on collection. Until it is paid, the vehicle stays with us.

What happens to the details you send us, and what Square does with a payment, is set out in our privacy policy.
