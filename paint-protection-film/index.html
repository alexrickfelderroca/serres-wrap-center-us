Why PPF in Florida

does to paint.

Every one of these is a South Florida problem before it is a paint problem. Film is the layer that takes them instead of your clear coat.

Six lanes of traffic flicking gravel at your hood and bumper on every commute. The film absorbs the strike; the paint under it never knows.

Florida sun works on clear coat all year, not for one summer. The film's top coat carries the UV load and keeps the gloss where it started.

Wind-blown sand sandblasts the leading edges — bumper, mirrors, A-pillars — and coastal salt sits on everything else. Those are the exact panels every coverage level starts with.

The install

Five steps,
no shortcuts.

The same protocol our Barcelona studio runs. Nothing gets skipped because a car is booked tight.

  1. 01

    Wash & decon

    Two-bucket wash, iron fallout remover, clay. Anything still on the paint ends up sealed under the film for years, so nothing is left on it.

  2. 02

    Paint inspection, thickness gauge

    We read the paint panel by panel with a thickness gauge and photograph what we find. Respray, filler or thin clear coat changes the plan — and you hear about it before we start, not after.

  3. 03

    HEPA-filtered install bay

    The car moves into a HEPA-filtered, anti-static bay. Airborne dust trapped under film is the single most common reason a panel has to come back off.

  4. 04

    Install

    Pattern cut for your exact model, edges wrapped in wherever the panel allows, filtered-air dry, then a panel-by-panel review under controlled light. A panel that is not right is redone before you see the car.

  5. 05

    Warranty & aftercare

    You leave with the manufacturer film warranty — up to 10 years — plus a 1-year SERRES installation warranty covering lifting, bubbling and edge failure, and a written aftercare sheet for the first wash, the edges and which chemicals to keep away.

Frequently asked questions

PPF, made clear.

What we get asked before protecting a car. If your case is different, message us on WhatsApp.

Will paint protection film turn yellow?

No. Both films we install are TPU, not the old PVC film that yellowed in the sun. Essential is NAR H190 PPF and Signature is 3M Scotchgard Pro Series 200, and each carries its manufacturer's own warranty against yellowing, cracking and staining. Manufacturer film warranty (up to 10 years) + 1-year Serres installation warranty.

Does the film really heal its own scratches?

Yes, within limits. The top coat is self-healing: swirl marks and light wash scuffs close up with heat from the sun or warm water. A deep gouge that cuts right through the film does not heal - but it stopped at the film instead of at your paint, which is the point.

What exactly does the warranty cover?

Manufacturer film warranty (up to 10 years) + 1-year Serres installation warranty. The film manufacturer covers the film itself against defects such as yellowing, cracking, bubbling and delamination. Our year covers the installation: lifting edges, trapped dirt, anything that is our work rather than the film.

How long does the car stay with you?

One to three days for most cars, depending on coverage. A partial front or full front is usually a single day. Track Pack and full body need two to three, because every panel is decontaminated and inspected before any film is laid. The exact number of days for your car comes with the quote.

How do I look after a filmed car?

Wash it like paint, just more gently. Hand wash with a pH-neutral shampoo, keep a pressure washer off the film edges, and leave polishes, waxes and anything abrasive out of it. A ceramic top-up over fresh film makes washing easier again: from $600.

Can the film be removed later?

Yes - film is designed to come off. With heat and slow, controlled pulling it lifts away without taking the clear coat with it, which is exactly why owners film a car before they sell it. Film left on long past its warranty life gets harder to remove, so do not leave a failing panel on the car.
