Founders Club terms

The small print,
in full.

All of it. There is no longer version of this held somewhere else.

  • The deposit is refundable in full until work begins on your car. Once the car is on the bench it stops being refundable, because the slot and the film are already committed.

Frequently asked questions

Before you
reserve.

What the club includes, what the deposit does, and how many cars one person can put in.

What exactly do the first thirty cars get?

Three things: 15% off any paint protection film or wrap, a free ceramic top-up over the fresh film, normally $600, and Founding Member status, which is 10% off detailing for life. The discount is locked to the price list in force on the day you book. “From” pricing = real price for a standard sedan in good condition — no bait pricing.

Is the deposit refundable, and what happens to it?

It is refundable in full until work begins on your car, and it is applied to your final invoice rather than charged on top. The amount is 100 US dollars. No card details are taken on this site: we send a secure deposit link by text or email once your slot is confirmed. Prices exclude FL sales tax.

Can I reserve more than one car?

One car per reservation, so thirty slots stay thirty cars. If you want a second car in the Founders Club, send a second reservation and we will confirm whether a slot is still open for it. Every car booked this way carries the same cover as any other job here: Manufacturer film warranty (up to 10 years) + 1-year Serres installation warranty.
