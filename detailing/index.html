How far we go

Stage 1. Stage 2.
Stage 3.

No two paints are alike. Our listed correction covers one to two stages; a third finishing pass is quoted separately when the clear coat needs it.

01

Stage 1 · Cut

Cutting pad · heavy compound

The aggressive stage — levelling deeper scratches, swirls and oxidation by removing a precise micron-thin layer of clear coat.

02

Stage 2 · Refine

Polishing pad · medium polish

The haze and micro-marring left by cutting are refined away, building back clarity and lifting gloss across the panel.

03

Stage 3 · Finish

Finishing pad · fine polish

The final mirror-finish (jewelling) pass eliminates holograms and brings the paint to a true, defect-free gloss before it is sealed in.

Frequently asked questions

Before you book.

What people ask before a detail — prices, timings and how we work in Boca Raton.

How much does detailing cost in Boca Raton?

There are two services and two numbers. An Interior Detail starts at $300 and a Paint Correction, one to two stage, starts at $600. “From” pricing = real price for a standard sedan in good condition — no bait pricing. Prices exclude FL sales tax.

What is included in the interior detail?

A full interior strip-down, hot extraction of carpets and upholstery, and leather cleaned and fed. Every panel, vent and seam is steamed and agitated, trim and glass are finished by hand, and the car goes back with nothing left to wipe. Vehicle size and how much has built up are what move the final number, and we confirm it at intake before any work starts.

What does paint correction do, and how many stages do I need?

Correction is machine polishing that removes defects rather than hiding them. Stage 1 cuts, levelling swirls, scratches and oxidation. Stage 2 refines away the haze that cutting leaves. Stage 3 is a final jewelling pass for a show finish. The listed price covers one to two stages, which is what most daily-driven cars need, and a third stage is quoted separately when the clear coat asks for it. Gloss is read with a meter before and after, so the result is a number: a typical neglected panel goes from about 42 GU on arrival to about 94 GU after correction. It is also the right prep step before a ceramic coating, so the finish is sealed at its best rather than as found.
