Frequently asked questions

About the numbers.

What people ask before they book, answered with the same figures printed above.

Are these the real prices, or a starting point to get me on the phone?

Real prices. “From” pricing = real price for a standard sedan in good condition — no bait pricing. Prices exclude FL sales tax.

Why does PPF have two prices on every line?

Because you choose the film. Essential is NAR H190 PPF: self-healing 7.5-mil TPU with a 10-year film warranty. Signature is 3M Scotchgard Pro Series 200, the 3M flagship. Partial Front is $1,000 in Essential and $1,150 in Signature; Full Front is $1,900 and $2,150. Same prep, same installers, same installation warranty — the film is the only difference.

What would move my quote away from the number on this page?

Vehicle size, panel shape and paint condition. A large SUV takes more film than a sedan, deep contours take longer to lay, and paint that needs correction before a coating adds work. Send the make, model and year and you get the exact figure for that car.

What is not priced on this page?

Roof, mirrors, spoilers, stripes and one-off requests are quoted per car, because the panel count and the shapes decide the price.
