How much does paint protection film cost?

Paint protection film starts at $1,000 for a partial front in NAR H190 and $1,900 for a full front. The 3M Scotchgard Pro Series 200 tier is a step up from there. “From” pricing = real price for a standard sedan in good condition — no bait pricing. Prices exclude FL sales tax.

What warranty do I get?

Manufacturer film warranty (up to 10 years) + 1-year Serres installation warranty. The installation warranty covers lifting, bubbling or edge failure caused by the fit, and it is ours, not the film maker's.
