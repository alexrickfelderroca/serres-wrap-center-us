Why film, in this city

Four roads
that cost you paint.

Paint protection film is a 7.5-mil TPU layer. Its job is to be hit instead of your clear coat.

  • I-95Glades to Yamato

    The commuter stretch, and the one that does the damage. Cones narrow a lane, everyone closes up, and whatever sits on the shoulder gets picked up by the truck ahead and put into your bumper at seventy. Film takes that hit in the TPU instead of your clear coat.

  • Federal HighwayUS-1 through east Boca

    Stop, start, stop. Landscaping trailers and loaded pickups at the exact following distance a red light creates. Low-speed stone strike lands on the bumper and the leading edge of the hood — which is what a partial front covers.

  • A1A and the beach lotsSand, salt and wiping

    Sand off the ocean settles as a fine grit on every horizontal panel, and then somebody wipes it away with a towel. Two seasons of that is a hazed hood and a machine polish that costs you clear coat. On film, you wash the film.

  • GaragesTown Center and Mizner Park

    Tight columns, valet stands, doors either side. Film does not stop a dent and we will not pretend otherwise. It stops the scuffing on a bumper corner, a door edge and the sill you step over — what makes a three-year-old car look tired.
