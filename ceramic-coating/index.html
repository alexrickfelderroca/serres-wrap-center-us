The Transformation

Water just
lets go.

Before coating, water lies flat across the panel and clings — dragging dust, minerals and road film into the surface as it slowly dries into spots.

Once our ceramic layer cures, the surface energy drops dramatically. Water can't wet the paint, so it pulls into beads and sheets away — taking dirt with it and leaving the gloss untouched.

The Process

Prep. Coat.
Cure.

01

Prep & Decon

Wash · clay · paint correction

Every defect is locked in forever once coated — so we decontaminate, clay and machine-correct the paint to a clean, swirl-free base first.

02

Application

SiO₂ ceramic · hand-levelled

The liquid coating is laid down panel by panel and levelled by hand in tight sections, bonding chemically with the clear coat as it flashes.

03

Cure

Controlled cure · 24h dwell

The coating is left to cross-link and harden in a dust-free, climate-controlled bay — setting into the slick, glass-hard shell.

Frequently asked questions

Before you book.

Straight answers on price, timelines and durability of the ceramic treatment.

How much does a ceramic coating cost in Boca Raton?

Two prices, both published. The 3-Year Ceramic Package on bare paint starts at $1,200. Over fresh paint protection film or a wrap, the top-up starts at $600. “From” pricing = real price for a standard sedan in good condition — no bait pricing. Prices exclude FL sales tax.

What does the 3-Year Ceramic Package include?

Decon, single-stage gloss prep, coating, cure. The prep is the part that decides the result: a coating locks in whatever is under it, so the paint is cleaned and corrected before anything is sealed onto it. The car goes back to you once the coating has cured, not straight off the bench.

Can a ceramic coating go over PPF or a wrap?

Yes, and it is the usual pairing. Ceramic over fresh film gives you the beading and the easier washing without touching the paint underneath, and it makes the whole car behave the same way instead of half film and half bare panel. That is the top-up, from $600.

Does a ceramic coating replace paint protection film?

No. A coating adds gloss, water beading and easier washing, but it is microns thick and it will not stop a stone chip. For impact protection you need film: from $1,000. Film on the front end and ceramic over the rest is how most cars leave the studio.

Do I need paint correction before the coating?

Some, always. The 3-Year Ceramic Package includes a single-stage gloss prep for exactly that reason. Heavier swirls, holograms or etching are a separate paint correction job, from $600, and it is worth doing first because a coating seals in every defect it goes over.
