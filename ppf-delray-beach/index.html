Getting here from Delray

South, and
not far.

Two ways down, and one of them is always moving.

Delray is directly north of the studio, so the run is south and it is short. The simple version is Federal Highway out of downtown: past the Atlantic Avenue lights, through the Linton Boulevard junction, then straight on US-1 until you reach us.

In season that is not always the fast version. When the Avenue is stacked between Swinton and the Intracoastal, or the bridge is up, the quicker line is Linton Boulevard west to I-95 and south from the ramp. Both end in the same place — take whichever one is moving.

Film work is a drop-off, not a wait. Coverage sets the length: a partial front is a short job, a full body is not. We tell you the day the car comes back at the point we quote it, so you plan around the car instead of the other way round.

Why film, here

What Delray
does to paint.

Four kinds of damage, and they do not arrive the same way.

01

Atlantic Avenue parking

Between Swinton and the bridge the kerbside spaces are short and the car behind you is impatient. Door edges, bumper corners and the sills take it. All of that is low-speed and cosmetic — exactly the damage a film layer absorbs instead of the clear coat.

02

Pineapple Grove and the side streets

Off the Avenue the streets narrow, the crossings change surface and there is grit wherever a truck has been working. That produces scuffing and fine swirling rather than impact. Self-healing film puts most of it back with heat instead of a polisher.

03

The I-95 commute

This is the expensive one. Everything loose between the Linton Boulevard ramp and Boca arrives at the front of the car at highway speed. A stone chip is not a detailing problem — it is through the clear coat and into the paint, and it does not polish out.

04

A mile from the water

Salt carries inland off the beach, the sun works on every horizontal panel it can reach, and love bugs turn up twice a year with an acid problem attached. Film takes that load first. Ceramic over the top of it makes the whole car let go of dirt.
