Getting here

Two exits,
not a road trip.

The shortest run on our map: a film drop-off costs a Pompano Beach driver a morning, not a day off.

Most Pompano cars reach us one of two ways. From east of the tracks, Atlantic Boulevard carries you inland to the I-95 ramp and the rest is two exits north. From the warehouse belt off Copans Road you are already pointed the right way.

Rather not touch the interstate? Follow Federal Highway up the coast instead: slower, full of lights, but one road all the way.

Why film, here

Four roads,
three kinds of damage.

A Pompano commuter, a plaza car and a beach car arrive with three different problems. Only two are film problems.

  • I-95, Atlantic to CopansStone chips · hood and fenders

    Years under cones. Lane shifts put you closer to the trucks than you would choose, and construction traffic leaves aggregate the car ahead flicks straight at your nose.

  • Copans RoadLow-speed impacts · bumper and valance

    The warehouse spine of north Broward: flatbeds, dump trucks, yard gravel, all at forty rather than seventy. Slow only changes the target — stones land low, on the bumper and valance.

  • Federal HighwayScuffs · corners, mirrors, door edges

    Lights, angled parking and plaza entrances from the Citi Centre lot on down. Nothing here chips paint; it scuffs it. A wrapped film edge shrugs that off.

  • The beach endSalt and sand · every panel

    Sand blows off the pier lot and across the Intracoastal bridge, and acts like a fine abrasive on any car wiped down dry. Salt does the slower damage.

Film answers the first three roads. For the fourth you want a coating and a hose, and we would rather say so than sell you a panel you do not need.

How far is the studio from Pompano Beach?

Closer than anywhere else we cover. The studio is in the Boca Raton and Pompano Beach area, so the run in is I-95 north from the Atlantic Boulevard or Copans Road ramp, or Federal Highway the whole way. The exact address and a measured drive time are published in October 2026.

Which PPF coverage suits a Pompano Beach car?

It depends which road the car lives on. An I-95 commuter past Copans Road collects chips on the hood and fender tops, so Full Front at $1,900 is the honest answer. A car that mostly does Federal Highway plazas and the Citi Centre lot picks up bumper corners and mirror caps, and Partial Front at $1,000 covers that.
